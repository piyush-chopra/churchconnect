## Product Purpose
Upload a résumé, discover relevant jobs, save opportunities, apply, and manage hiring.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Candidate and employer accounts; private résumé storage; PDF, DOCX and TXT extraction; transparent keyword skill matching; employer job posting and applicant review; responsive installable PWA. Local SQLite development database, Django API, React client. Native store distribution, email delivery, payments and third-party AI services are outside this first implementation.
